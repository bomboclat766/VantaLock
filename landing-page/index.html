Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored exclusively in an encrypted file on your local computer (`app.getPath('userData')`). VantaLock does not own servers to store your information.
What happens if I lose my Master Password?
During initial setup, VantaLock generates a 24-word BIP-39 recovery seed phrase. You can restore full access to your vault using this phrase. If you lose both, no one can recover your data.
Can I export and back up my vault?
Yes. VantaLock supports fully encrypted 1-click backups that can be exported and imported on any device.
