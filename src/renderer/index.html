Welcome to VantaLock Encrypted Vault

Greetings! Welcome to VantaLock — your sovereign personal vault. Built for total privacy, zero telemetry, and complete local hardware security.

Where is my vault stored?
Exclusively on your local device using military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption derived with Argon2id.
Does VantaLock connect to cloud servers?
Never. Except for optional license checks, VantaLock never transmits data to any network or server.
What if I forget my master password?
You will generate a 24-word recovery seed phrase during setup. That phrase is your only key if lost.
